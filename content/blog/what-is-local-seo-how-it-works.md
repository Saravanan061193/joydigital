If you own a physical store, a local medical clinic, or provide services in a specific geographic area, standard SEO is not enough. You need **Local SEO**. 

Local SEO is the process of optimizing your online presence to attract more business from relevant local searches on Google and Google Maps.

In this beginner's guide, we explain what Local SEO is and how the local search algorithm works.

## Why Local SEO Matters for Small Businesses

When users search for services on their mobile phones, Google displays the **Local 3-Pack**. This is a highlighted map block showcasing three local businesses that match the query. 

Most mobile searches have local intent (e.g., 'dentist near me' or 'plumbing services in Chennai'). If your business does not appear in these top three maps spots, you are missing out on direct calls, directions requests, and walk-in customers.

## How Google's Local Search Algorithm Works

Unlike general web searches, Google's local search algorithm evaluates results based on three primary factors:

### 1. Relevance
Relevance measures how closely your business profile details match the user's search query. Choosing the correct primary business category, writing detailed keyword descriptions, and listing all services on your Google Business Profile are essential to boost relevance signals.

### 2. Distance
Distance evaluates how far your physical business address is from the searcher's location (or the location name specified in the query). While you cannot change your physical location, optimizing for surrounding zip codes can help expand your ranking radius.

### 3. Prominence
Prominence measures your business's offline and online authority. Google evaluates prominence using:
* **Directory Citations:** Having your Name, Address, and Phone number (NAP) listed consistently across web directories.
* **Customer Reviews:** The quantity, ratings, and frequency of reviews on your Google Business Profile.
* **Website Backlinks:** Links from trusted regional websites and industry portals.

## Actionable Steps to Improve Your Local Rankings

1. **Claim Your Google Business Profile:** Ensure your profile is verified and all details are complete.
2. **Standardize Your NAP Details:** Keep your address and phone number spelling identical across the web.
3. **Set Up a Review Acquisition Plan:** Make it easy for happy clients to leave positive reviews.
4. **Add Local Business Schema:** Inject structured location data on your homepage so Google's web crawlers can read your exact address and geo-coordinates.
