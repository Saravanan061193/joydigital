If you are planning to build a website for your business, one of the first questions you will probably ask is:

**“How much will a website cost in India?”**

The honest answer is: **it depends on what you actually need.**

A small business website and a large e-commerce website are completely different projects. The number of pages, design, features, technology, content, SEO, maintenance and integrations can all affect the final price.

In this guide, we’ll break down the typical website costs in India in 2026 so you can understand what you are paying for before choosing a website development company.

## Website Cost in India in 2026

For a small or medium-sized business, website development can generally fall into these broad ranges:

| Type of Website               |   Typical Cost Range |
| ----------------------------- | -------------------: |
| Basic Business Website        |    ₹8,000 – ₹20,000+ |
| Professional Business Website |   ₹20,000 – ₹50,000+ |
| Custom Business Website       | ₹50,000 – ₹1,00,000+ |
| E-commerce Website            | ₹30,000 – ₹1,50,000+ |
| Custom Web Application        | ₹75,000 – ₹3,00,000+ |

These are general market ranges, not fixed prices. A project can cost less or considerably more depending on its requirements.

So instead of asking only **“What is the cheapest website?”**, it is better to ask:

**“What type of website does my business actually need?”**

---

## 1. Basic Business Website – ₹8,000 to ₹20,000+

This is usually suitable for a small business that simply wants an online presence.

For example:

* Local service businesses
* Freelancers
* Consultants
* Small shops
* Insurance agents
* Pest control companies
* Tours and travel businesses
* Individual professionals

A basic website might include:

* Home page
* About Us
* Services
* Contact page
* WhatsApp button
* Contact form
* Google Maps
* Basic mobile responsive design

If your main goal is to let customers find your business online and contact you, this may be enough.

However, don't expect a low-cost website to include every advanced feature.

---

## 2. Professional Business Website – ₹20,000 to ₹50,000+

If your website is going to represent your brand seriously, you may want something more professional.

A professional business website can include:

* Custom UI/UX design
* Multiple service pages
* Mobile and desktop optimization
* WhatsApp integration
* Contact forms
* Google Maps integration
* Basic SEO setup
* Social media integration
* Speed optimization
* Google Analytics/Search Console setup
* Better conversion-focused layouts

For many growing businesses, this is the range where you start getting a website that is not just an online visiting card but also works as a **lead generation tool**.

---

## 3. Custom Website – ₹50,000 to ₹1,00,000+

Some businesses need more than standard pages.

For example, you may need:

* Custom calculators
* Customer login
* Appointment booking
* Product catalogues
* Advanced enquiry forms
* CRM integration
* Payment gateway
* API integrations
* Custom dashboards
* Advanced animations
* Custom admin panels

At this point, the development work becomes more customized, so the cost naturally increases.

The price is usually based on the features and development time rather than simply the number of pages.

---

## 4. E-commerce Website – ₹30,000 to ₹1,50,000+

Planning to sell products online?

Then you need more than a normal business website.

An e-commerce website may require:

* Product management
* Categories
* Product search
* Shopping cart
* Checkout
* Payment gateway
* Order management
* Customer accounts
* Shipping integration
* Coupons and discounts
* Inventory management
* Admin dashboard

A small online store may be built at a relatively lower cost, while a large store with custom requirements can cost significantly more.

If you have hundreds or thousands of products, multiple sellers, complex shipping or custom integrations, the budget will increase further.

---

# What Actually Determines Website Cost?

The price is not just about “designing a few pages.”

Several things affect the final cost.

## 1. Number of Pages

A 5-page website is obviously different from a website with 30 service and location pages.

More pages mean more:

* Content
* Design
* Development
* SEO work
* Testing

---

## 2. Design

There is a big difference between using a basic template and creating a custom design.

If you want your website to look different from your competitors, custom UI/UX design will usually require more time and budget.

A good website should also be easy to use.

A beautiful website that visitors cannot navigate easily is not a good website.

---

## 3. Features

Every additional feature can affect development cost.

For example:

**Simple contact form:** lower complexity.

**Appointment booking + payment + customer login + admin management:** much higher complexity.

This is why two websites with the same number of pages can have completely different prices.

---

# What About Domain and Hosting?

Domain and hosting are separate parts of the website budget.

For example:

**Domain:**
Your business name such as `yourbusiness.com`

**Hosting:**
The server where your website files are stored.

Depending on the technology and hosting provider, hosting costs can vary.

Some website companies include domain and hosting for the first year as part of their package, while others charge them separately.

Before choosing a package, always ask:

**“What is included after the first year?”**

This can save you from unexpected renewal costs later.

---

# Is SEO Included in Website Development?

This is one of the most important questions to ask.

Some companies build the website and stop there.

But building a website does not automatically mean people will find it on Google.

A basic website should at least have proper:

* Page titles
* Meta descriptions
* Heading structure
* Image alt text
* Clean URLs
* Mobile responsiveness
* Sitemap
* Robots.txt
* Internal links
* Basic technical SEO

If you want your website to rank for competitive keywords, you will need an ongoing SEO strategy.

That is a separate investment from simply building the website.

---

# Why Are Some Websites So Cheap?

You may see advertisements like:

**“Website ₹2,999”**

or

**“Website ₹5,000”**

That doesn't necessarily mean the company is bad.

It may simply be a very basic package.

But before comparing prices, check what is actually included.

Ask:

* Is the design custom or template-based?
* How many pages are included?
* Is domain included?
* Is hosting included?
* Is SSL included?
* Is mobile optimization included?
* Is SEO included?
* Can I update the website later?
* Is support included?
* What happens after one year?
* Are there additional charges for changes?

A ₹10,000 website with everything included may actually be better value than a ₹5,000 package with multiple additional charges.

---

# How Much Should a Small Business Budget for a Website?

If you are a small business owner in India, you don't necessarily need to spend lakhs on your first website.

For many businesses, a **well-planned ₹15,000–₹50,000 website** can be a reasonable starting point, depending on the features and level of customization required.

The important thing is to spend money on the things that actually help your business:

**Good design + clear information + mobile experience + SEO basics + easy customer contact.**

Don't spend ₹1 lakh on features you will never use.

At the same time, don't choose the cheapest possible option if your website is going to be an important part of your business.

---

# Website Cost Is Not the Same as Website Value

This is something many business owners miss.

Suppose you spend ₹20,000 on a website and it brings you 10 new customers.

That's very different from spending ₹10,000 on a website that nobody can find and nobody understands.

Your website should ideally help with:

* Brand credibility
* Customer enquiries
* Google visibility
* WhatsApp enquiries
* Lead generation
* Product/service information
* Customer trust

So instead of looking only at the development price, think about the **business value the website can create**.

---

# Questions to Ask Before Hiring a Website Development Company

Before you pay an advance, ask these questions:

### 1. Who owns the domain?

Make sure the domain is registered in your business name or that you have proper access.

### 2. Will I get access to my website?

Ask whether you will receive the necessary hosting, domain and admin credentials.

### 3. Is the website mobile-friendly?

Most customers will access your website from a phone, so this is essential.

### 4. Is SEO included?

Ask exactly what “SEO included” means.

### 5. How many revisions are allowed?

This avoids confusion during the design stage.

### 6. What happens after the website is launched?

Ask about maintenance, support and yearly renewal charges.

---

# So, How Much Does a Website Really Cost in India?

There isn't one fixed price.

A basic business website might cost around **₹8,000–₹20,000+**, while a professional or custom website can move into the **₹20,000–₹1,00,000+** range.

E-commerce websites and custom web applications can cost considerably more.

The right budget depends on your business goals, required features and how important the website is to your customer acquisition strategy.

The best approach is simple:

**Decide what your business needs first. Then choose the technology and budget.**

Don't start with:

> “What is the cheapest website I can get?”

Start with:

> **“What website will actually help my business get more customers?”**

That question will usually lead you to a much better decision.
