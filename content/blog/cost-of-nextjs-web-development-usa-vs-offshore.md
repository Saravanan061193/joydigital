## Why Is US Local Development So Expensive?

US digital agencies carry substantial operational overhead:

1. **High Hourly Developer Rates**: Senior US Next.js engineers bill between **$125 and $220 per hour**.
2. **Administrative & Sales Overhead**: Up to 40% of a US agency's proposal fee goes toward sales teams, account executives, and commercial office rents.
3. **Subcontracting Markup**: Many US agencies quietly white-label their backend work to offshore developers while charging clients US domestic rates.
