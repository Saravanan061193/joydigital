## Website Speed: The Silent Ranking Factor in Google Search

In today's fast-paced digital world, users expect information instantly. If your business site takes too long to render, prospects will exit.

So, **how fast should a corporate website load to rank on Google?**

According to Google search guidelines and user behaviour audits, a website should load in **under 2 seconds**. If a page takes longer than 3 seconds to load on mobile connections, the probability of visitors bouncing increases by over **32%**.

Let's explore the technical speed benchmarks and how modern frameworks help you meet them.

---

### The Crucial Speed Benchmarks
Google measures page rendering metrics using three Core Web Vitals parameters:
1. **Largest Contentful Paint (LCP):** Measures how fast the main visual blocks render. *Target: under 2.5 seconds.*
2. **First Input Delay (FID) / Interaction to Next Paint (INP):** Measures page interactivity and button click responsiveness. *Target: under 200 milliseconds.*
3. **Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS):** Measures page layout shifts during load events. *Target: score under 0.1.*

---

## Why Site Load Speed Impacts Organic Traffic

Google uses page experience as an active search engine ranking factor. Slow sites suffer from two major SEO bottlenecks:
* **High Bounce Rates:** When users exit a slow page immediately, Google's crawlers interpret it as a poor user experience and lower your search rank.
* **Reduced Crawl Budget:** Search engine crawlers spend less time indexing bloated, slow platforms, meaning your new articles or services updates take longer to appear in search index listings.
