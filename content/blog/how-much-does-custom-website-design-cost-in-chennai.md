## Understanding Website Design Price in Chennai

When building an online presence, one of the most common questions small businesses and startups ask is: **"How much does a custom website design cost in Chennai?"**

The short answer is that website design price in Chennai starts from **₹15,000** for starter business portfolios and can go up to **₹75,000+** for complex, custom database platforms or e-commerce applications.

However, the final pricing depends heavily on the code architecture, page count, and custom features. Let's break down the components of website pricing.

---

### 1. Starter Portfolios (Under ₹25,000)
For local service providers, LIC agents, financial advisors, or small clinics, a simple 1 to 5-page responsive website is the best option. 
* **Average Cost:** ₹15,000 – ₹25,000
* **Typical Features:** Responsive templates, contact forms, click-to-call, WhatsApp chat integration, and Google Maps embed.
* **Best For:** Creating a digital business card and capturing direct local inquiries.

### 2. Business Growth Sites (₹25,000 – ₹50,000)
For growing companies that want to rank for local keywords, write blogs, and capture leads systematically, a larger, SEO-optimized platform is required.
* **Average Cost:** ₹25,000 – ₹50,000
* **Typical Features:** Up to 10 custom pages, complete SEO metadata architecture, local JSON-LD schema injection, Google Analytics tracking, and priority support.
* **Best For:** Clinic and hospital sites, hotels, educational hubs, and tours & travels agencies.

### 3. Custom Enterprise & E-commerce (₹50,000+)
For brands that require online booking portals, payment gateway integrations, custom databases, or custom admin control panels.
* **Average Cost:** ₹50,000 – ₹1,00,000+
* **Typical Features:** Unlimited static pages, Razorpay/Stripe checkout systems, custom database management, premium caching CDNs, and monthly support plans.
* **Best For:** Online retail stores, real estate listing engines, and corporate portals.
