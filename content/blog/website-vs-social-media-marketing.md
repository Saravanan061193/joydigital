When launching a new business or planning a marketing budget, one of the most common questions is: *Should I invest in a custom website, or focus on social media marketing?*

While both channels are valuable, they serve different purposes. In this guide, we compare the pros, cons, and ROI of websites vs. social media marketing.

## Website Marketing: Ownership & Conversions

A website is your custom digital real estate. It operates as your official corporate office online.

### Pros:
* **Complete Ownership:** You control the layout, branding, and user journey without algorithm restrictions.
* **Higher Conversion Rates:** Websites are optimized for lead capture (forms, booking links, and call buttons).
* **Sustainable SEO Traffic:** Ranking on Google brings in high-intent visitors who are actively searching for your services.

### Cons:
* **Higher Initial Setup Cost:** Building a custom React/Next.js site requires developer fees.
* **Maintenance Needed:** Requires domain renewals, hosting setups, and security checks.

## Social Media Marketing: Engagement & Outreach

Social media platforms (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn) are great channels to build community interest.

### Pros:
* **Immediate Outreach:** Easy to share visual updates, reels, and chat with users.
* **Low Entry Barrier:** Setting up business profiles is free and instant.
* **Ad Targeting Options:** Paid social campaigns allow you to target detailed interests and demographics.

### Cons:
* **Zero Real Ownership:** You do not own the platform. If the algorithm updates, your organic reach can drop.
* **Low Conversion Control:** It is difficult to set up customized lead funnels within the app.

## The Verdict: Integrate Both for Maximum Growth

Choosing one over the other is not the best approach. A successful digital marketing strategy integrates both:

1. Use **Social Media** to build brand awareness, publish Reels, run paid lead ads, and drive initial interest.
2. Direct that traffic back to your **Custom Website** where you can display metric showcases, detailed FAQs, client testimonials, and capture enquiries professionally.
